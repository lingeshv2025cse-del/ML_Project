What this system does

It detects matching businesses across different source systems, preserves multiple valid matches, and exports a decision-ready result set.

How it works

The pipeline preprocesses CSV inputs, blocks likely matches, creates similarity features, predicts a match score, and applies configurable thresholds.

Why it matters

Real-world business records are noisy, incomplete, and formatted differently. Fuzzy matching and ML improve accuracy and explainability.
